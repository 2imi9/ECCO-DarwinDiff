# Pre-registration: a self-twin to decide which loss comparison is correct

**Date:** 2026-08-08 · **Submitted before results are seen.** · **Arms:** 15 × 50 seeds
(4 loss×target cells × 3 basins, + 3 untrained nulls) · **Script:**
`scripts/slurm/run_loss_twin.sbatch` · **Levers:** `TWIN_TARGET`, `TWIN_ANCHORS`,
`TIME_MEAN_LOSS` · **Config:** `scripts/configs/flagship_geo1.sh`, sourced, with a runtime
assert on six values including `N_STEPS=200` and `NB23_LR=5e-3`.

## 1. The question

Every published `scav_rat` number rests on a comparison that was never deliberately made. The
loss scores the model's **instantaneous state at day 50** against a **time-averaged Darwin
target** (`time_mean(ds_aoi)`). Those are different objects in the time dimension; the endpoint
comparison was inherited, not chosen.

Making them commensurable does not improve or degrade the fit — it **selects which basin
identifies `scav_rat`**. Single-AOI, all three collapses, replicated on disjoint seeds
(288619 / 289204): eqpac **1/50 → 50/50**, southernoceanpac **49/50 → 0/50**.

Three readings fit that evidence and **nothing measured so far separates them**:

- **(a)** the correction is right; `scav_rat` is identifiable in the equatorial Pacific — the
  basin with twice the iron data — and the established Southern Ocean result is the artifact;
- **(b)** the correction introduces its own error, since a mean over 0–50 days of an unconverged
  trajectory is not a Darwin climatological mean either, and the eqpac result is the artifact;
- **(c)** both losses are wrong in different ways, each least wrong in one basin.

Three candidate mechanisms have already been tested and refuted: vertical iron structure,
per-basin endpoint-vs-time-mean distance, and long-run-proxy quality.

## 2. Why a twin, and why this twin

A self-twin removes forward-model misspecification entirely. The target is generated by the box
itself at Carroll truth, so **"recovered" means "recovered the truth"** rather than "matched
Darwin". The grading machinery needs no change: it already grades against Carroll, and in a twin
Carroll *is* the truth.

`TWIN_ANCHORS=1` regenerates the real-observation anchors (GEOTRACES surface/subsurface Fe,
Daniels CP:PP, GEOTRACES bSi) from the **same** twin trajectory, sampled at their **own** cells,
so coverage, masks and normalisers stay real and only the values become twin values. This is
load-bearing, not cosmetic — measured locally before submission, `R_PICPOC`'s relative offset is
**17.253** in a pattern-only twin and **0.711** once the anchors are regenerated. The reason is
structural: z-scoring removes the uniform scaling `R_PICPOC` applies, so a pattern-only twin
cannot see it at all.

The runner **fails loudly** rather than mixing target kinds: any real-observation term left
active without `TWIN_ANCHORS=1` aborts the run, because scoring a Carroll-generated box against
the real ocean reintroduces exactly the misspecification the twin exists to remove.

## 3. The design, and its built-in falsifier

|                  | loss = endpoint        | loss = time-mean       |
|------------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| `target=end`     | **MATCHED — must pass** | (not in this submission) |
| `target=mean`    | (not in this submission) | **MATCHED — must pass** |
| `target=long`    | **DECISIVE**            | **DECISIVE**            |

`long` integrates to `TWIN_LONG_STEPS=4000` (1000 days), the quasi-steady fixed point. It is the
honest analogue of the real target: a Darwin climatology is a mean of a **quasi-steady** system,
not a mean over a 50-day spin-up from a Darwin initial condition. Under the box's constant
forcing, a trailing mean at quasi-steady equals the fixed point, so the endpoint at long time is
the correct construction and no extra averaging is needed.

## 4. Pre-registered predictions

**P1 — harness falsifier. The two MATCHED cells must recover the truth.** They compare identical
objects, so a failure there means the twin is broken. **If either matched cell fails to recover
`alpfe` and `scav_rat` in a basin, no conclusion may be drawn about that basin's decisive
cells.** This is the rule two previous window sweeps violated by inferring from an arm whose
control had already failed.

**P2 — directional, and refutable. Against the `long` target, the ENDPOINT loss recovers truth
better than the time-mean loss.** Section 7 of the 2026-08-06 loss finding measured the endpoint
closer to the fixed point in **all three basins** (eqpac 6.783 vs 10.475, natl 9.517 vs 15.178,
SO 6.050 vs 9.580), and for every tracer converged by 200 steps the endpoint *is* the long-run
value at relative distance 0.00 while the 50-day mean is contaminated by spin-up. If P2 holds,
reading **(b)** is supported and the eqpac time-mean result is the artifact. **If the time-mean
loss wins against `long`, P2 is refuted and reading (a) is supported** — the outcome that
contradicts my own prior.

**P3 — the basin swap is NOT predicted, and both outcomes are decisive.** If the twin reproduces
the eqpac↔SO swap, the swap is intrinsic to box dynamics under a comparison choice, and reading
(c) gains support. If the twin does *not* reproduce it, the swap comes from the real Darwin
target — misspecification, not dynamics — and the twin has localised it. Neither is the
hoped-for answer.

**P4 — `target=long` will degenerate the Chl1 (diatom) pattern target.** Under constant forcing
the diatom goes extinct and its long-run value is the clamp floor, so the field has no spatial
contrast. `_twin_z` detects this, prints it, and neutralises the term. **The `long` arms
therefore carry one fewer pattern term than the `end` arms, and that asymmetry will be stated in
the write-up rather than hidden.** It is also a reason `diatomgraz` is not gradeable in the
`long` cells and will not be reported there.

**P5 — `R_PICPOC` is a machinery check, not a result.** It is recoverable in the twin only
through the regenerated Daniels anchor. A `R_PICPOC` failure with anchors on indicates a broken
anchor regeneration, not a scientific finding.

## 5. Decision rule, fixed in advance

- Grade on the **per-AOI ≥2-of-3 rule is NOT used here** — every arm is single-AOI, so each arm
  is graded on its own basin's leg, against that basin's untrained null.
- `scav_rat` is read under the **geometric** collapse, with arithmetic and median reported
  beside it. Never arithmetic alone.
- Report the **per-basin median as ×Carroll**, not only counts. A count that moves without the
  median moving is a band effect.
- **Band sensitivity at ±0.05** before any claim.
- "Recovers truth" for the matched cells means the count clears its untrained null at
  P < 0.01 **and** the median sits within the 0.40 band.

## 6. Falsifiers that can kill the whole submission

1. **Either matched cell fails** → the twin harness is broken; report that and nothing else.
2. **All four cells recover truth equally well in all three basins** → the twin is insensitive to
   the comparison choice, and the question cannot be settled this way. This is a real possible
   outcome: the twin removes misspecification, and if the swap is *caused* by misspecification,
   the twin will show no swap and no preference. Reported as a null.
3. **The nulls pass** → the band is doing the work, not the fit; grade at a tighter band or
   report the arm as uninformative.

## 7. ADDENDUM — measured after submission, before any fit result exists

Job 320993 was submitted at 04:20 UTC and is queued behind 108 running jobs with an estimated
start of 09:12 UTC. **No arm has produced a single number.** Everything below is a property of
the twin TARGETS, computed by integrating the box and measuring the fields — no fit involved —
so it can be stated now without any possibility of retro-fitting.

**P4 is REFUTED as stated, and its mechanism produces the opposite pathology.** No target was
flagged degenerate in any basin at `long`. The diatom does not settle to a uniform clamp floor.
It goes extinct *heterogeneously*, which drives its relative spatial sd the other way:

| relative spatial sd | `Chl1` end → long | `co2_flux` end → long | **`DFe2` end → long** |
|---|---|---|---|
| eqpac | 0.241 → **4.035** | 0.277 → 1.235 | 0.0845 → **0.0001** |
| natlsubpolar | 0.499 → **8.202** | 0.141 → 1.399 | 0.1323 → **0.0026** |
| southernoceanpac | 0.688 → **6.074** | 0.105 → 0.963 | 0.2390 → **0.0006** |

Two things follow, and neither was anticipated.

**1. The `long` target is nearly information-free for `scav_rat`.** `DFe2` is the field
`scav_rat` is anchored on, and its spatial contrast collapses by **845×, 51× and 398×** between
the 200-step endpoint and the fixed point. At quasi-steady state the box's subsurface iron is
spatially almost uniform. A pattern term over a uniform field carries no signal, so the two
decisive cells may fail for `scav_rat` **in both losses**, for a structural reason that has
nothing to do with the endpoint-versus-time-mean question.

That does not void the experiment; it sharpens what a null there would mean. If both `long`
cells fail while both matched cells pass, the result is not "the twin is insensitive" — it is
**a quasi-steady climatological target cannot constrain `scav_rat` at all**, which would place
the parameter's entire identifiability in the transient and support reading (b) in a stronger
form than originally posed. That reading is now pre-registered too.

**2. A limitation of the twin that the design did not account for.** Section 3 argued that
under constant forcing a trailing mean at quasi-steady equals the fixed point, so the endpoint
at long time is the right construction. That arithmetic is still correct, but it exposes the
real gap: **the box is forced constantly and the actual Darwin climatology is a mean of a
SEASONALLY forced system, which never reaches a fixed point.** So `long` is an analogue of a
steady climatology, not of Darwin's. The honest statement is that this twin brackets the
comparison question between two constructions the box can produce, and neither is Darwin's
target exactly. A seasonally-forced twin is the follow-on, not a repair of this one.

`Chl1`'s relative sd of 4 to 8 means its mean is far below its spread, so the term becomes
close to a binary mask over the surviving cells and will be disproportionately weighted in the
z-scored pattern loss. `diatomgraz` was already excluded from the `long` cells by P4; this is
the reason it stays excluded, and the reason `Chl1`-driven behaviour in those cells is not
evidence about anything.

## 8. What is deliberately NOT in this submission

- The two remaining mismatched cells (`end`×time-mean, `mean`×endpoint). They quantify the cost
  of a mismatch but are not decisive, and the submission is already 75 tasks.
- Any change to the flagship. `TWIN_TARGET` defaults to `off` and every published number is
  produced with it off.
- Replication. This is **one submission**; a `SEED_BASE=50` re-run on disjoint seeds is the
  follow-on, and until it lands no result here may be reported as replicated.
