14 / The summer's durable contribution

An observing-system design result, expressed through differentiable modeling.

1 / Absolute anchors remove scale ambiguity

`R_PICPOC` succeeds because an external calcite anchor reaches it; a dust source anchor cannot identify an iron sink rate.

2 / Vertical structure can separate source from sink

Depth-resolved iron changes local Fisher rank; conditioning is not itself recovery.

3 / Regions expose different process parameters

`scav_rat` and `diatomgraz` are identifiable in different basins and fail to globalize.

4 / Current time-mean targets lack growth information

Seasonality may recover one growth direction that this climatological design cannot see.

5 / Compute moves practical boundaries

Epochs and throughput can improve optimization, but cannot repair absent observations or a failed target.

6 / Evidence control is part of the method

Poolers, matched nulls, supersessions, and falsifiers determine what a recovery count means.
