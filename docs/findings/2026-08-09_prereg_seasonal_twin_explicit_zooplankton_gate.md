# Preregistration: source-mirrored explicit-zooplankton seasonal target gate

**Frozen before implementation or measurement: 2026-08-09.** This is a
bounded Stage-0 target-construction test for issue #239. It runs no optimizer
and authorizes no B200 work unless every gate below passes. The relational-map
queries

```text
python scripts/research_map_db.py settled "density-dependent grazing seasonal twin"
python scripts/research_map_db.py settled "explicit zooplankton seasonal twin"
```

returned no settled answer over 539 untruncated SETTLED rows.

## Why this test is next

The already-verified process budget and rare-invasion experiments localize the
current seasonal target failure to the diatom loss closure. At Carroll's truth,
the box applies a biomass-independent diatom-specific loss rate
`M_LIN + diatomgraz * G0_GRAZE = 0.299009 d-1`. The annual rare-diatom
multiplier is negative in all three AOIs under astronomical light even though
North Atlantic and Southern Ocean cells have favorable individual seasons.
Another light or restoring-timescale sweep would therefore vary a nonbinding
or already-bounded lever.

Carroll's source model has a materially different mechanism: two explicit
zooplankton biomasses graze all five phytoplankton types through a shared prey
pool and a Holling-II response. `diatomgraz` multiplies diatom palatability; it
is not itself a constant specific mortality. The exact source lineage frozen
for this prototype is:

- `MITgcm-contrib/ecco_darwin`, Carroll-2020 baseline
  `code_darwin/darwin_plankton.F`: prey-weighted two-predator grazing;
- v04 JAMES `darwin_init_fixed.F`: `kgrazesat=0.085` in phosphorus units,
  `GrazeFast=GrazeSlow=0.625 d-1`, `ZoomortSmall=ZoomortBig=1/30 d-1`,
  `palathi=1`, `palatlo=0.2`, `coccograz=0.85`, and
  `olargegraz=0.90`;
- Carroll baseline `data.traits`: the same half-saturation converted to
  carbon units (`10.2 mmol C m-3`), two predator assimilation matrices, and
  no zooplankton temperature multiplier.

This prototype is a source-mirrored projection into the existing 5-PFT
surface box, not a claim to reproduce all 39 Darwin-1 tracers.

## Frozen model

### State and forcing

- Retain all 15 production states unchanged.
- Add two surface carbon-biomass states: `Z_small` and `Z_large`. There is no
  subsurface biology in the current two-layer box, so no L2 grazer is added.
- Use only the already-verified parameter-free astronomical monthly-light
  construction. The fixed-light arm is not eligible for selection.
- Use the existing monthly SST, SSS, wind, atmospheric CO2, Darwin chemical
  initial-condition caches, `dt=0.25 d`, and `122` steps per month.
- Restore only the ten already-preregistered chemical/particle states toward
  their frozen references with `tau=365.25 d`. Never restore phytoplankton or
  zooplankton.
- Integrate 12 cycles and evaluate cycles 11 and 12. No extension will be made
  after seeing the result.

### Grazing algebra

For predator `z`, prey `p`, prey biomass `P_p`, predator biomass `Z_z`, and
palatability `a_pz`:

```text
B_z       = sum_p a_pz P_p
I_z       = Gmax Z_z B_z / (B_z + K_graze)
G_pz      = I_z a_pz P_p / max(B_z, epsilon)
prey_loss = sum_z G_pz
zoo_gain  = sum_p assimilation_pz G_pz
```

with `Gmax=0.625 d-1`, `K_graze=10.2 mmol C m-3`, and
`zoo_mortality=1/30 d-1`. The tiny source prey threshold is represented only
by a numerical denominator guard and cannot be tuned.

The PFT order is `(diatom, other-large-euk, syn, proLL, proHL)`. The frozen
palatability columns `(small grazer, large grazer)` are:

```text
diatom          (0.2 * diatomgraz, 1.0 * diatomgraz)
other-large-euk (0.18,                 0.90)
syn             (1.00,                 0.20)
proLL           (1.00,                 0.20)
proHL           (1.00,                 0.20)
```

The frozen assimilation columns are `(0.2,0.5)`, `(0.2,0.5)`, and
`(0.5,0.7)` for diatom, other-large-euk, and each small PFT respectively, as
generated by the Carroll size-class rules. Explicit grazing replaces the
box's linear diatom-only grazing term. Assimilated prey enters zooplankton;
unassimilated prey and zooplankton mortality enter POC. Routing the dissolved
fraction to POC is the declared projection forced by the box's lack of a DOC
state. The test must report this limitation and may not call the prototype a
full Darwin port.

### Initial-condition robustness

Let `P_total` be the initial sum of the five phytoplankton states in each cell.
Each zooplankton state is initialized to `f_z P_total` for all three frozen
fractions:

```text
f_z in {0.01, 0.10, 0.50}
```

The central target is `f_z=0.10`, but it is ineligible unless the endpoint is
robust to both flanking initializations. No measured Darwin zooplankton field
is used as a hidden target or restoring reference.

## Frozen gates

All gates are evaluated over verified ocean cells separately in Eq Pacific,
North Atlantic subpolar, and Southern Ocean Pacific. One failed AOI fails the
construction.

1. **Numerical integrity.** All states and diagnostics are finite. The
   biology clamp correction is below 1% of gross biological turnover and raw
   negative events occur in below 0.1% of biology state-updates.
2. **Cycle stability.** Every existing Stage-0 field plus both zooplankton
   fields has cycle-11-to-12 masked relative L2 change `<=0.01`.
3. **Chemical closure.** Every restored state retains the existing gross
   closure-share `<=0.50` and mean-inventory-turnover `<=1.0` gates. Every
   phytoplankton and zooplankton closure increment is exactly zero.
   Threshold comparisons allow `1e-6` absolute floating-point tolerance; the
   reported values remain unrounded.
4. **DFe structure.** The existing per-AOI DFe2 relative-spatial-SD minima and
   `<=5%` cycle-to-cycle contrast-change gate pass.
5. **Diatom viability.** Cycle-12 mean diatom inventory is at least 1% of its
   initial inventory and its minimum monthly AOI-mean is strictly positive.
   This is a persistence gate, not an accuracy claim.
6. **Community viability.** Each of the other four PFTs and both predators
   retains at least 1% of its own initial mean inventory. No one plankton state
   exceeds 95% of total plankton carbon in the cycle-12 mean.
7. **Seasonality.** The central target's AOI-mean diatom monthly coefficient
   of variation is at least 0.05 in every AOI.
8. **Initial-condition robustness.** For every plankton state, the cycle-12
   all-step mean from each flank differs from the central target by masked
   relative L2 `<=0.05`.
9. **Parameter handle.** Rebuild the central target at `0.9x` and `1.1x`
   Carroll `diatomgraz`, changing no other value. In at least two AOIs, the
   absolute log response of cycle-12 mean diatom inventory across the two
   perturbations must be `>=log(1.05)`, and the response signs must agree
   across all AOIs with finite nonzero inventories. This is sensitivity, not
   recovery.
10. **Independent verification.** A separate verifier recomputes source
    algebra, mass partition, hashes, and every decision from the raw tensor
    bundle without importing runner decision helpers.

## Decision table
