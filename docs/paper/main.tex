Are Identifiable Through a Differentiable Surrogate?}

\begin{abstract}
Calibrating the biogeochemistry of a coupled general circulation model (GCM) such
as ECCO-Darwin v05 is hard because the model is not differentiable: its six
``Carroll-6'' calibration parameters are tuned by costly Green's-functions sweeps
(\(\sim\!7\) forward runs per gradient) rather than by backpropagation
\cite{menemenlis2005,carroll2022}. We ask which of these parameters are
\emph{identifiable} when the GCM is replaced, for inversion only, by a 0-D
differentiable surrogate (the \texttt{carroll6\_5pft\_2layer} box) fit jointly to
Darwin output and to absolute real-ocean anchors (GEOTRACES iron, a Daniels
PIC:POC ratio). A per-cell Darwin-Informed neural network predicts a local
Carroll-6 vector at each grid cell and backpropagates through the box; one
backward pass yields exact gradients of the \emph{surrogate} objective for all six
parameters (replacing Green's functions only as an inversion-side estimator, and
inheriting the surrogate gap below). Of six parameters, four are observable
\emph{in principle}; the \emph{demonstrated}-observable set is the trio
\(\{\)alpfe, scav\_rat, R\_PICPOC\(\}\), while diatomgraz observability remains
open (it is at-chance from the available real data). The growth pair (Smallgrow,
Biggrow) is unobservable by construction. An ablation isolating only the parameter
source shows the per-cell network is load-bearing for \emph{in-band recovery of}
\texttt{scav\_rat} and \texttt{R\_PICPOC}: on \(\{\)alpfe, scav\_rat, R\_PICPOC\(\}\)
it holds 7/10 jointly (\(n{=}10\) seeds, cross-confirmed) versus 0/10 for a single
global-scalar vector (Fisher \(p{<}0.01\)), because a global vector cannot match
spatially varying absolute anchors. \texttt{alpfe} recovers 10/10 in \emph{both}
arms, so it is mass-balance-identified (its magnitude pinned by the absolute iron
anchor), not per-cell-dependent. The global arm posts a \emph{higher} overall mean
recovery (mean \(n_{\mathrm{cal}}=4.0\) versus per-cell \(3.2\)), but only by
regularizing the \emph{unobservable} growth parameters toward their global
values---so the 7/10-versus-0/10 gap lives specifically on the target trio. We
characterize a surrogate gap---the box cannot carry circulation-driven spatial
structure---so identifiability must come from real, Darwin-independent anchors, not
pattern matching, extending the BINN design \cite{xu2025binn} to a
non-differentiable model. Separately, this is a single-method, single-model study
without an independent inversion, so recovery against Carroll's own values is a
consistency check, not a cross-validated discovery
\cite{heimbach2015,transtrum2012}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Coupled ocean biogeochemical general circulation models (GCMs) such as
ECCO-Darwin~\cite{carroll2022} are calibrated through a small set of governing
parameters whose values are fixed by expensive iterative procedures. The
operational state of the art is Green's-functions calibration, in which each
candidate parameter perturbation requires a full forward integration of the
coupled model; obtaining the gradient of a six-parameter calibration set
(hereafter ``Carroll-6'') against an objective therefore costs on the order of
seven forward ECCO-Darwin runs. The more powerful native inverse machinery---the
ECCO adjoint and its 4D-Var data-assimilation system~\cite{heimbach2015,
menemenlis2005}---is not available for the Darwin biogeochemistry: the coupled
GCM is, for practical purposes, \emph{non-differentiable} with respect to its
biogeochemical parameters. This rules out gradient-based parameter learning on
the model that practitioners actually run.

We ask whether a cheap, fully differentiable \emph{surrogate} can recover the
GCM's calibration parameters when the GCM itself cannot be differentiated. Our
surrogate is a zero-dimensional differentiable box model
(\texttt{carroll6\_5pft\_2layer}) sharing the Carroll-6 parameterization with
ECCO-Darwin v05. A per-cell Darwin-Informed Neural Network (DINN)---a stack of
$1{\times}1$ convolutions---predicts a local Carroll-6 vector for every grid
cell from its environment, and gradients backpropagate through the
differentiable box. A single backward pass yields all six exact gradients
\emph{of the surrogate objective}; this replaces the $\sim$7 forward
ECCO-Darwin runs of a Green's-functions sweep only as an inversion-side gradient
estimator, and the gradients it returns are gradients of the box, not of
ECCO-Darwin---so the estimate inherits the surrogate gap characterized below.
We fit the surrogate jointly to ECCO-Darwin v05 output \emph{and} to real
ocean observations (GEOTRACES IDP2025 dissolved iron, a Daniels CP:PP / MODIS
calcite ratio anchor) across three areas of interest (equatorial Pacific, North
Atlantic, Southern Ocean).

\paragraph{The identifiability question, not a recovery score.}
The central finding is that fitting the observable is \emph{not} the same as
identifying the parameter, and that identifiability is parameter-specific. We
therefore do not report a headline ``six-of-six'' score; we decompose the
Carroll-6 set into what is observable, what is not, and why. Four parameters are
in principle observable---iron-dust solubility \texttt{alpfe} and iron
scavenging \texttt{scav\_rat} (the ``iron pair''), diatom palatability
\texttt{diatomgraz}, and the PIC:POC rain ratio \texttt{R\_PICPOC}---but the
\emph{demonstrated}-observable set is the trio \{\texttt{alpfe},
\texttt{scav\_rat}, \texttt{R\_PICPOC}\}; \texttt{diatomgraz} is
observable-in-principle-but-not-from-the-staged-data (it is at-chance from the
available real observations, see below). The growth
pair (\texttt{Smallgrow}, \texttt{Biggrow}) is unobservable by construction:
there are no real growth-rate observations, and a 23-year climatology averages
away the seasonal-succession signal that would otherwise constrain it. We
report against the three demonstrated-observable parameters
\{\texttt{alpfe}, \texttt{scav\_rat}, \texttt{R\_PICPOC}\} plus
\texttt{diatomgraz} (observable in principle but not from the staged data), and
state the growth pair's status explicitly rather than counting it toward or
against any score.

\paragraph{The surrogate gap.}
A zero-dimensional box cannot carry the circulation-driven spatial structure of
a three-dimensional GCM, and we characterize this gap rather than paper over it.
At uniform parameters the box relaxes to a near-uniform state---tracer
coefficient of variation falls from $4\times10^{-5}$ at 200 steps to
$\sim10^{-15}$ at 6400 steps, against Darwin's $O(1)$ CV of 0.6--2.4. The gap has
two distinct components. (a) A \emph{dimensional / pattern} gap: the CV collapse
means box-versus-Darwin spatial-pattern correlation is \emph{not} a fidelity
metric, so identifiability must come from real, absolute, Darwin-independent
anchors rather than from matching the model's own spatial pattern. (b) An
\emph{intensive closure} gap that survives spatial averaging: the box's surface
PIC:POC is $\approx0.0424$ \emph{everywhere}, whereas Darwin's
\emph{ratio-of-means} (not mean-of-ratios) per AOI is $0.033$ (eqpac), $0.676$
(natl), and $0.0067$ (SO). The single box value $0.0424$ therefore sits
\emph{between} the two extremes with opposite-sign errors: it is $1.3\times$ too
high in eqpac, $16\times$ too \emph{low} in natl, and $6\times$ too \emph{high}
in SO. Because a single global scalar cannot be simultaneously raised toward
$0.676$ (natl) and lowered toward $0.0067$ (SO), no single-scalar closure can fit
all three AOIs---a stronger proof of the single-scalar closure limit than a
one-sided magnitude error. Gap (b) thus caps any joint multi-AOI
\texttt{R\_PICPOC} claim.

\paragraph{What makes the architecture load-bearing.}
Holding the configuration fixed and varying \emph{only} the parameter
source---per-cell DINN versus a single global-scalar Carroll-6 vector, with
per-cell forcing and initial conditions present in both arms---the per-cell
predictor holds the target trio \{\texttt{alpfe}, \texttt{scav\_rat},
\texttt{R\_PICPOC}\} jointly $7/10$ across seeds versus $0/10$ for the global
scalar (Fisher exact $p<0.01$ for \texttt{scav\_rat}, \texttt{R\_PICPOC}, and the
joint; cross-confirmed by a fresh identical-config re-run, both $7/10$).
\texttt{scav\_rat} and \texttt{R\_PICPOC} require per-cell prediction for in-band
recovery because a single global vector cannot match spatially varying absolute
anchors---global \texttt{R\_PICPOC} drifts to $0.092\approx2.2\times$ the Carroll
value $0.0425$ (this $0.092$ is the mean across seeds). We
state the nuance plainly: the global scalar's rigidity regularizes the
growth/grazing parameters toward their global Carroll values
(\texttt{Smallgrow}/\texttt{Biggrow}/\texttt{diatomgraz} $10/10$ versus per-cell
$1/4/1$ within a generous $\sim$40\% band), so its higher mean recovery
(mean $n_{\mathrm{cal}}=4.0$ versus per-cell $3.2$) is misleading---those
are unobservable non-target parameters that per-cell flexibility overfits, and the
$7/10$-versus-$0/10$ gap lives specifically on the target trio.
\texttt{alpfe} is recovered $10/10$ by both arms, so it is mass-balance-identified
(its magnitude pinned by the absolute iron anchor), not per-cell-dependent; only
\texttt{scav\_rat} is the iron parameter that requires per-cell prediction.

\paragraph{Identifiability is parameter-specific, and we show why.}
A synthetic test with a spatially varying truth makes the mechanism explicit: a
per-cell DINN drives the loss to $\sim10^{-4}$ yet recovers \texttt{alpfe} with
an \emph{arbitrary sign} across seeds ($n=6$ seeds; $3$ of $6$ recovered
\texttt{alpfe} with negative sign, so $|r|\sim0.9$ but the direction is
arbitrary), while a
parameter with a direct monotonic effect (\texttt{Smallgrow}) recovers
consistently---fitting the observable does not identify the parameter. Note the
two roles \texttt{alpfe} plays: in this synthetic test the truth is a
spatially varying field with \emph{no} absolute-magnitude anchor on
\texttt{alpfe}, so its \emph{sign} is free; the real GEOTRACES anchor instead
pins \texttt{alpfe}'s \emph{magnitude} (this is the mass-balance handle). The iron
pair is identified by mass balance, not iron-limitation physics: the recovered
operating point is near-saturated ($f_{\mathrm{Fe}}\sim0.67$--$0.91$), so
\texttt{alpfe} is pinned by dissolved-iron mass balance against the absolute
GEOTRACES IDP2025 anchor rather than by iron-limitation dynamics; the real-iron
likelihood profile is shallow (a direction, not a sharp value), and recovery
required up-weighting the real iron term $\sim10\times$ (GEOTRACES weight
$0.3\to3$)---the Darwin-pattern block alone does not identify the pair, which
strengthens the dimensional-gap claim. \texttt{R\_PICPOC} recovers via a real
ratio anchor: an anchor-off control scatters ($3/10$) while anchor-on
concentrates at $\approx0.05$ (the real Daniels value) ($10/10$, Fisher
$p=0.003$). \emph{Any} ratio
anchor recovers it, so the real anchor's contribution is non-circularity---it
ties recovery to a real $\approx0.05$ rather than to Darwin's own PIC:POC---not
recoverability itself. Crucially, $0.05$ is only $+18\%$ over Carroll's $0.0425$
(well inside the $40\%$ band), so the non-circularity rests on the anchor
\emph{source} (real calcite, not Darwin's own output), not on numeric distance.
Symmetrically with the global arm---whose growth $10/10$ is a rigidity prior
coinciding with the truth---the per-cell \texttt{R\_PICPOC} recovery is itself
\emph{anchor-imposed} (to $\approx0.05$); the load-bearing claim is precisely that
per-cell is needed to satisfy a \emph{spatially-varying} absolute anchor without
the single-scalar drift to $0.092$, not that the value is independently
identified. We report this as validation within a wide band
($\approx0.05$ versus Carroll $0.0425$, a $\sim$40\% calibration band) alongside
the caveat that Carroll's single global value is under-constrained and that gap
(b) caps a joint claim; we do \emph{not} claim to validate $0.0425$. A
self-twin control against box-generated targets at the known optimum recovers all
six parameters at loss $\sim10^{-10}$ when started at truth, confirming the
machinery is correct and that the from-mid-bounds stall is an optimization /
initialization artifact, not intrinsic degeneracy. \texttt{diatomgraz}
observability remains open: it is not recovered from available real observations
(best $\sim4/10$, at chance) and would in principle require dense Darwin
POSi/TRAC16 fields that we have not staged. This decomposition places the work in
the sloppiness and structural-identifiability lineage~\cite{transtrum2012},
where loss-insensitive (``sloppy'') parameter directions are the rule rather
than the exception in multiparameter models.

\paragraph{Relation to BINN.}
The closest prior work is the Biogeochemistry-Informed Neural Network of
Xu et al.~\cite{xu2025binn}, the mature published originator of the design we
build on: a per-location neural network whose predictions pass through a
differentiable biogeochemical model so that gradients train the network
end-to-end. The decisive difference is that BINN differentiates the
\emph{actual} model (CLM5) and therefore has \emph{no surrogate gap}; its
objective is self-consistency of a differentiable model with data. Our setting
is the opposite and harder one. (i) We transfer the design to a
\emph{non-differentiable} coupled GCM (ECCO-Darwin) by interposing a 0-D
differentiable surrogate, which introduces---and forces us to characterize---the
surrogate gap above. (ii) We replace the self-consistency lens with an
\emph{identifiability decomposition}: which parameters are observable and why,
rather than whether a differentiable model can fit itself. (iii) Identifiability
is driven by real, absolute, Darwin-independent anchors with multi-AOI
attribution, not by the model's own output. We do not use the native inverse
machinery that would be the conventional route---ECCO adjoint / 4D-Var
\cite{heimbach2015, menemenlis2005} and Green's-functions calibration---precisely
because it is unavailable for the Darwin biogeochemistry; positioning against it
is part of our contribution.

\paragraph{Scope and limitations.}
We state the limitations up front because they bound every claim that follows.
The 0-D box is a 1-degree \emph{proxy} for the 3-D GCM; the targets are a 23-year
climatology, not time-resolved; experiments run on a single-GPU prototype. Most
importantly, this is a single-method, single-model study: we run no independent
inversion (Green's-functions, Bayesian, or 4D-Var) on the same targets and no
forward-Darwin held-out validation, so ``recovery'' graded against Carroll's own
published values is a \emph{consistency check}, not a cross-validated discovery---this
is the single biggest limitation. The intensive closure gap (b) caps any joint
multi-AOI \texttt{R\_PICPOC} claim; \texttt{diatomgraz} observability is open; and
the growth pair is unobservable by construction. Every recovery number reported
here is re-derived from raw outputs and gated by an automated reproducibility
check (\texttt{verify\_run.py}); we stress that this is a data-integrity gate, not
a validation of the science. Conclusions were pre-registered, verification was
adversarial, and two self-corrections are on the record.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Problem setting and limitations}

ECCO-Darwin v05 \cite{carroll2022} is a coupled ocean biogeochemistry general circulation model (GCM) calibrated through a Green's-functions procedure: each parameter perturbation requires a separate forward integration, and the model is not differentiable end-to-end. Our goal is not to replace that calibration but to ask a narrower, falsifiable question: which of the six Carroll-6 calibration parameters can be \emph{identified} from output and observations alone, and why. We frame our approach against the native inverse machinery we do not use---the ECCO adjoint and 4D-Var system \cite{heimbach2015,menemenlis2005} and Green's-functions calibration---and against the parameter-sloppiness lineage \cite{transtrum2012} that supplies our notion of structural identifiability.

We state the load-bearing limitations up front. The surrogate is a $0$-D box that acts as a one-degree \emph{proxy} for the 3-D GCM; it is fit to a 23-year climatology and is therefore not time-resolved; and the prototype runs on a single GPU. Most importantly, this is a \emph{single-method, single-model} study: we run no independent inversion (Green's-functions, Bayesian, or 4D-Var) on the same targets and no forward-Darwin held-out validation. ``Recovery'' graded against Carroll's own published values is therefore a \emph{consistency check}, not a cross-validated discovery---this is the single biggest limitation. Of the six parameters, only four are observable in principle---\texttt{alpfe} (iron-dust solubility), \texttt{scav\_rat} (iron scavenging), \texttt{diatomgraz} (diatom palatability), and \texttt{R\_PICPOC} (PIC:POC rain ratio); the demonstrated-observable set is the trio \{\texttt{alpfe}, \texttt{scav\_rat}, \texttt{R\_PICPOC}\}, while \texttt{diatomgraz} is observable in principle but not from the staged data. The growth pair \{\texttt{Smallgrow}, \texttt{Biggrow}\} is unobservable by construction, as no real growth-rate observations exist and a 23-year climatology averages away the seasonal-succession handle that would constrain it. We carry an \emph{honest denominator} of three demonstrated-observable parameters plus \texttt{diatomgraz} (observable-in-principle) throughout.

\paragraph{The Cal-grade recovery band.}
All recovery counts in this paper are reported at a single, pre-registered threshold we call \emph{Cal-grade}: a parameter counts as recovered if its recovered value lands within a $40\%$ relative offset of the published Carroll value. The exact convention used by the code carries finer sub-bands---``Excellent'' ($\le5\%$), the $40\%$ Cal-grade threshold itself, ``Loose'' ($\le80\%$), and ``Drifted'' ($>80\%$)---but every count below is graded at the single $40\%$ Cal-grade threshold. We stress that this band is \emph{generous}, and that it is a project convention for in-band recovery, \emph{not} a Carroll or Green's-functions tolerance: it is not an accepted error bar on the Carroll values, only a fixed yardstick we register in advance and apply uniformly to every recovery count.

\subsection{Differentiable $0$-D surrogate}

The surrogate is the \texttt{carroll6\_5pft\_2layer} box: a $0$-D, five-phytoplankton-functional-type, two-layer differentiable forward model implemented so that each Carroll-6 parameter is an explicit, gradient-bearing input. Replacing the GCM with a differentiable box transfers the inverse problem from a non-differentiable model to one in which a single backward pass yields all six exact gradients \emph{of the surrogate objective}, versus the roughly seven forward ECCO-Darwin Green's-functions integrations required to estimate the same sensitivities by perturbation. We are careful here: the backward pass replaces Green's functions \emph{only} as an inversion-side gradient estimator, and the gradients it returns are gradients of the box objective, not of ECCO-Darwin---so the estimate inherits the surrogate gap below. We therefore do not claim a like-for-like speedup of the GCM calibration itself.

This transfer is not free. A $0$-D box cannot carry circulation-driven spatial structure, and we characterize the resulting \emph{surrogate gap} rather than conceal it. At uniform parameters the box relaxes to a spatially near-uniform state: tracer coefficient of variation falls from $4\times10^{-5}$ at 200 integration steps to $\sim10^{-15}$ at 6400 steps, against Darwin's $O(1)$ spatial CV of $0.6$--$2.4$. We split the gap into two parts. (a)~A \emph{dimensional/pattern gap}: the CV collapse above, which makes box-vs-Darwin spatial-pattern correlation \emph{not} a fidelity metric and forces identifiability to come from real, absolute, Darwin-independent anchors. (b)~An \emph{intensive closure gap}: the box's surface PIC:POC is $\approx 0.0424$ everywhere, while Darwin's \emph{ratio-of-means} (not mean-of-ratios) is $0.033/0.676/0.0067$ across the three areas of interest. The single box value $0.0424$ sits \emph{between} the extremes with opposite-sign errors: $1.3\times$ too high in eqpac, $16\times$ too \emph{low} in natl, and $6\times$ too \emph{high} in SO. A single global scalar cannot be simultaneously raised toward $0.676$ (natl) and lowered toward $0.0067$ (SO), so no single-scalar closure can fit all three AOIs---a stronger proof of the single-scalar closure limit than a one-sided magnitude error. Gap~(b) is therefore a structural single-scalar closure limit that caps any joint multi-AOI \texttt{R\_PICPOC} claim, and we report it as such.

\subsection{Per-cell DINN and the architectural ablation}

The estimator is a per-cell Darwin-Informed Neural Network (DINN): a stack of $1\times1$ convolutions that maps each grid cell's local environment to its own Carroll-6 vector. Gradients backpropagate from the loss through the differentiable box into the convolutional weights, so the network learns a spatially varying parameter field rather than a single global setting.

To test whether this per-cell flexibility is load-bearing, we introduce a \texttt{GlobalScalarNet} control that predicts a single global Carroll-6 vector shared by every cell, and ablate the two arms under an identical configuration (with per-cell forcing present in both). The comparison and its results are reported in Sec.~\ref{sec:results-ablation}.

\subsection{Loss and real, absolute anchors}

Because the surrogate gap rules out spatial-pattern matching as a fidelity signal, identifiability must be driven by real, absolute, Darwin-independent anchors. The loss combines Darwin-output terms with two real-observation anchors.

\emph{Iron (GEOTRACES IDP2025).} The recovered iron operating point is near-saturated ($f_{\mathrm{Fe}} \approx 0.67$--$0.91$), so \texttt{alpfe} is identified by dissolved-iron \emph{mass balance} against the absolute GEOTRACES anchor, not by iron-limitation dynamics; we reframe the iron pair accordingly. We are precise about what each setting constrains: the absolute GEOTRACES anchor pins \texttt{alpfe}'s \emph{magnitude} (this is the mass-balance handle, and the reason \texttt{alpfe} recovers $10/10$ in both arms); the synthetic sign-flip control of Sec.~\ref{sec:results-equifinality} uses a spatially-varying truth with \emph{no} absolute-magnitude anchor on \texttt{alpfe}, leaving its \emph{sign} free; neither experiment claims to constrain \texttt{alpfe}'s full spatial field. ``Mass-balance-identified'' therefore means ``magnitude pinned.'' The real-iron likelihood profile is shallow---it constrains a direction, not a sharp value---and recovery required up-weighting the real-iron term roughly $10\times$ (\texttt{GEOTRACES\_W} from $0.3$ to $3$). The Darwin-pattern block alone does not identify the iron pair, which strengthens the dimensional-gap claim: the signal that resolves \texttt{alpfe} is the real absolute anchor, not the box's reproduction of Darwin structure.

\emph{Calcite (Daniels CP:PP / MODIS).} \texttt{R\_PICPOC} recovers via a real ratio anchor. The anchor's role is \emph{non-circularity}, not recoverability: an anchor-off control recovers \texttt{R\_PICPOC} in only $3/10$ runs with scattered values, while anchor-on recovers $10/10$ at $\approx 0.05$ (the real Daniels value, Fisher $p=0.003$), and any ratio anchor recovers it. The real anchor's value ties \texttt{R\_PICPOC} to the observed $\approx 0.05$ rather than to Darwin's own PIC:POC. Note that $0.05$ is only $+18\%$ over Carroll's $0.0425$ (well inside the $40\%$ band), so the non-circularity rests on the anchor \emph{source}---real calcite, not Darwin's own output---and not on numeric distance from Carroll. Symmetrically with the global arm, whose growth $10/10$ is a rigidity prior coinciding with the truth, the per-cell \texttt{R\_PICPOC} recovery is itself \emph{anchor-imposed} (to $\approx0.05$); the load-bearing claim is that per-cell is needed to satisfy a \emph{spatially-varying} absolute anchor without the single-scalar drift to $0.092$, not that the value is independently identified. Recovery is validation \emph{within a wide band}---$\approx 0.05$ against Carroll $0.0425$, within the $\sim\pm40\%$ Cal band---and we report it alongside the facts that Carroll's single global value is under-constrained and that closure gap~(b) caps a joint multi-AOI claim. We do \emph{not} claim recovery validates the value $0.0425$.

\subsection{Identifiability is parameter-specific: synthetic and self-twin controls}

Fitting the observable is not the same as identifying the parameter, and we demonstrate this with two controls. In a \emph{synthetic equifinality} test with a spatially varying ground truth, the per-cell DINN drives the loss to $\sim10^{-4}$ yet recovers \texttt{alpfe} with an \emph{arbitrary sign across seeds} ($|r|\sim0.9$, sign-flip, $n=6$ seeds; $3$ of $6$ seeds recovered \texttt{alpfe} with negative sign), while \texttt{Smallgrow}, whose effect on the observable is directly monotonic, recovers more consistently---identifiability is parameter-specific even when the fit is excellent. Because this synthetic truth carries no absolute-magnitude anchor on \texttt{alpfe}, only its sign is unconstrained; it is the real GEOTRACES anchor, not this control, that pins \texttt{alpfe}'s magnitude.

As a \emph{method-isolation} self-twin, we replace the real and Darwin targets with box-generated targets at the known Carroll optimum. Starting at truth, the same machinery recovers all six parameters at loss $\sim10^{-10}$. The method therefore works as an estimator; the from-mid-bounds stall observed on real targets is an optimization/initialization artifact, not intrinsic degeneracy of the estimator.

\subsection{\texttt{diatomgraz} and the integrity gate}

\texttt{diatomgraz} observability remains open: it is not recovered from the available real observations (best $\sim4/10$, at chance), and is recoverable in principle only from dense Darwin POSi/TRAC16 fields, which we have not staged.

Every recovery number reported above is re-derived from raw outputs and gated by \texttt{verify\_run.py}, which recomputes each statistic from raw and exits zero only on agreement. We emphasize that this is a \emph{data-integrity} gate, not validation: it guarantees that reported numbers trace to raw runs, not that the recovered parameters are physically correct. Conclusions were pre-registered, verification was adversarial, and two self-corrections remain on the record.

\subsection{Relation to BINN}

The per-location-network-through-a-differentiable-BGC-model design originates with the Biogeochemistry-Informed Neural Network (BINN) of \cite{xu2025binn}, which differentiates the \emph{actual} model (CLM5) and therefore has no surrogate gap. Our contribution is threefold. (i)~We transfer the design to a \emph{non-differentiable} GCM (ECCO-Darwin) through a $0$-D differentiable surrogate, which introduces and forces us to characterize the surrogate gap. (ii)~We adopt an \emph{identifiability-decomposition} lens---asking which parameters are observable and why---rather than demonstrating self-consistency. (iii)~Our identifiability is driven by real absolute anchors with multi-AOI attribution, not by agreement with the model's own output.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

We organize the evidence around a single question: when the target GCM cannot be
differentiated, which of its six Carroll-6 calibration parameters does a 0-D
differentiable surrogate, fit to GCM output \emph{and} real ocean observations,
actually identify? We separate the demonstrated-observable trio
$\{\texttt{alpfe}, \texttt{scav\_rat}, R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}\}$ and the
observable-in-principle \texttt{diatomgraz} (not recoverable from the staged data)
from the growth pair $\{\texttt{Smallgrow}, \texttt{Biggrow}\}$, which is
unobservable by construction: no real growth-rate observations exist, and the
23-yr climatology averages away the seasonal-succession signal that would
otherwise constrain growth rates. Every recovery count below is re-derived from
raw outputs and gated by an automated data-integrity check; this gate enforces
reproducibility of the numbers, not validity of the conclusions. Unless noted,
counts are over $n{=}10$ seeds and ``recovery'' means a marginal lands inside the
single pre-registered, generous $40\%$ ``Cal-grade'' band around the published
Carroll value (a project convention for in-band recovery, not a Carroll or
Green's-functions tolerance; see Sec.~\ref{sec:method}).

\subsection{The per-cell architecture is load-bearing for in-band recovery of the target trio}
\label{sec:results-ablation}

Our central comparison holds the entire pipeline fixed and varies only the
\emph{source} of the Carroll-6 vector. Both arms use the identical \texttt{geo1}
configuration (three areas of interest---eqpac, natl, SO---with real GEOTRACES
iron and the real Daniels calcite anchor) and, critically, both arms receive the
\emph{same} per-cell forcing and initial conditions. The per-cell arm lets a
Darwin-Informed neural network (a stack of $1{\times}1$ convolutions) predict a
Carroll-6 vector at every grid cell from local environment, backpropagating
through the differentiable box; the control arm fits a single \emph{global-scalar}
Carroll-6 vector to the same data. Because per-cell forcing is present in both
arms, this isolates the parameter field itself: it directly answers the
objection that ``a global-scalar box with per-cell forcing is not flat.''

On the target trio $\{\texttt{alpfe}, \texttt{scav\_rat}, R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}\}$,
the per-cell DINN recovers all three \emph{jointly} in 7/10 seeds; the
global-scalar control recovers them jointly in 0/10
(Table~\ref{tab:ablation}). The marginals locate the failure precisely:
\texttt{alpfe} recovers in both arms (10/10 vs.\ 10/10), but \texttt{scav\_rat}
(7/10 vs.\ 0/10) and $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ (9/10 vs.\ 0/10) require the per-cell
field. A fresh, identically-configured re-run reproduces the joint result
(7/10 in both runs). Fisher's exact test gives $p<0.01$ for \texttt{scav\_rat},
for $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$, and for the joint trio. The mechanism is that a single
global vector cannot match spatially-varying \emph{absolute} anchors: the
global-scalar $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ drifts to $0.092 \approx 2.2\times$ the
Carroll value of $0.0425$ ($0.092$ is the mean across seeds). We are careful about
what this establishes: the ablation shows per-cell prediction is \emph{necessary}
for \texttt{scav\_rat} and $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ to land in-band against the
spatially-varying absolute anchors; it does not by itself establish point
identification (cf. the sign-flip control of
Sec.~\ref{sec:results-equifinality}).

This necessity-not-identification reading is borne out by the across-seed spread
of the recovered \emph{values} (not merely the in-band counts). The global-scalar
arm is sharply convergent but systematically wrong: across the ten seeds its
recovered values have coefficient of variation $\mathrm{CV}\approx0\%$ for all
three trio parameters, yet that single optimum sits outside the band for
\texttt{scav\_rat} ($41\%$ from Carroll) and $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ ($118\%$, the
drift to $0.092$)---one global vector that cannot be both raised and lowered to
meet spatially-varying anchors. The per-cell arm is the mirror image: its mean
recovered values sit near the targets (\texttt{alpfe} $2.9\%$ and \texttt{scav\_rat}
$9.6\%$ from Carroll; $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ at $\approx0.053$, the real Daniels
value), but the across-seed spread is wide for \texttt{scav\_rat}
($\mathrm{CV}\approx43\%$)---it reaches the band $7/10$ of the time without being
sharply point-identified, while $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ is the sharpest per-cell
value ($\mathrm{CV}\approx10\%$), consistent with being pinned by the absolute
calcite anchor. The honest reading is therefore that per-cell prediction is what
lets \texttt{scav\_rat} and $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ reach the band at all (the
global optimum is sharply \emph{outside} it), but only $R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$
approaches sharp identification---and even then to the anchor's value, not
Carroll's.

\subsection{The global arm's higher mean recovery is misleading}
\label{sec:results-nuance}

The same comparison carries a nuance we state rather than hide. The
global-scalar's rigidity acts as a regularizer that pins the growth and grazing
parameters near their global Carroll values, so the control recovers
\texttt{Smallgrow}, \texttt{Biggrow}, and \texttt{diatomgraz} at 10/10 each,
against the per-cell arm's 1/10, 4/10, and 1/10 (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}).
Consequently the global arm posts a \emph{higher} mean count of recovered
parameters (mean $n_{\mathrm{cal}} = 4.0$ vs.\ the per-cell arm's $3.2$; the
per-cell mean is $(10+7+9+1+4+1)/10 = 3.2$ and the global mean is $40/10 = 4.0$).
This is an artifact of the denominator: those three
are \emph{non-target} parameters---the growth pair is unobservable by
construction and grazing observability is open (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-diatomgraz}).
The per-cell field's flexibility \emph{overfits} them, while the global arm's
``recovery'' is merely a rigidity prior coinciding with the truth. The mean is
misleading precisely because the $7/10$-versus-$0/10$ gap lives specifically on
the target trio, not in this count. The
parameter-by-parameter reading is the honest one: \texttt{alpfe} is recovered by
both arms because it is mass-balance-identified (its magnitude pinned by the
absolute iron anchor; Sec.~\ref{sec:results-iron}),
not per-cell-dependent; \texttt{scav\_rat} is the iron parameter that genuinely
requires the per-cell field; and the trio result, not the mean count, is the
load-bearing claim.

\subsection{A dimensional surrogate gap, in two parts}
\label{sec:results-gap}

The 0-D box cannot reproduce the GCM's spatial structure, and we decompose this
gap into a dimensional/pattern part and an intensive closure part.

\paragraph{(a) Dimensional/pattern gap.}
At uniform parameters the box relaxes to a spatially near-uniform state: the
tracer coefficient of variation collapses from $4\times10^{-5}$ at 200 integration
steps to ${\sim}10^{-15}$ at 6400 steps, against Darwin's $\mathcal{O}(1)$
spatial CV of $0.6\text{--}2.4$. The box carries no circulation-driven structure.
The direct consequence is that box-vs-Darwin spatial-\emph{pattern} correlation is
\emph{not} a fidelity metric and cannot serve as an identification signal;
identifiability must come from real, absolute, Darwin-independent anchors.

\paragraph{(b) Intensive closure gap.}
A second gap survives spatial averaging, and it is \emph{two-sided}. The box
surface PIC:POC sits at ${\approx}0.0424$ essentially everywhere, whereas Darwin's
\emph{ratio-of-means} (not mean-of-ratios) is
$0.033$ (eqpac), $0.676$ (natl), and $0.0067$ (SO) per AOI. The single box value
$0.0424$ thus sits \emph{between} the two extremes with opposite-sign errors: it
is $1.3\times$ too high in eqpac, $16\times$ too \emph{low} in natl, and
$6\times$ too \emph{high} in SO, independent of any spatial pattern. This is a
structural single-scalar closure limit, and the two-sidedness sharpens the
argument: a single global rain ratio cannot be simultaneously raised toward
$0.676$ (natl) and lowered toward $0.0067$ (SO), so no single-scalar closure can
fit all three AOIs---a stronger proof of the single-scalar closure limit than a
one-sided magnitude error. It therefore \emph{caps any joint multi-AOI claim} on
$R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$.

\subsection{The iron pair is identified by mass balance, not iron-limitation physics}
\label{sec:results-iron}

We reframe the iron-pair result honestly. The recovered operating point is
near-saturated in the iron-limitation term ($f_{\mathrm{Fe}} \approx
0.67\text{--}0.91$), so \texttt{alpfe} is not constrained by iron-limitation
\emph{dynamics} but by dissolved-iron \emph{mass balance} against the absolute
GEOTRACES IDP2025 anchor. We are explicit about what is constrained: the absolute
anchor pins \texttt{alpfe}'s \emph{magnitude} (and this is why \texttt{alpfe}
recovers 10/10 in both arms---``mass-balance-identified'' means ``magnitude
pinned''); the synthetic control of Sec.~\ref{sec:results-equifinality}, which
carries no absolute anchor, leaves \texttt{alpfe}'s \emph{sign} free; neither
experiment constrains its full spatial field. Consistent with this, the real-iron
likelihood profile is shallow---it fixes a direction, not a sharp value. Recovery
of the pair required up-weighting the real iron term by ${\sim}10\times$
($\texttt{GEOTRACES\_W}: 0.3 \rightarrow 3$); the Darwin-pattern block
\emph{alone} does not identify the pair. This strengthens, rather than weakens,
the dimensional-gap claim of Sec.~\ref{sec:results-gap}: identification comes from
the real absolute anchor, not from matching Darwin's spatial pattern.

\subsection{$R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$: the real anchor buys non-circularity, not recoverability}
\label{sec:results-rpicpoc}

$R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ recovers through a real ratio anchor (Daniels CP:PP /
MODIS), and the key control shows that \emph{any} ratio anchor recovers it: with
the anchor off, recovery is 3/10 and scattered; with the anchor on, recovery is
10/10 at ${\approx}0.05$ (the real Daniels value, Fisher $p=0.003$). The real
anchor's contribution is
therefore \emph{non-circularity}---it ties the recovered ratio to a real-world
value of ${\sim}0.05$ rather than to Darwin's own internal PIC:POC---and not
recoverability per se. Note that $0.05$ is only $+18\%$ over Carroll's $0.0425$
(well inside the $40\%$ band), so the non-circularity rests on the anchor
\emph{source} (real calcite, not Darwin's own output), not on numeric distance
from Carroll. Symmetrically with the global arm---whose growth 10/10 is a rigidity
prior that happens to coincide with the truth---the per-cell
$R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ recovery is itself \emph{anchor-imposed} (to ${\approx}0.05$):
the load-bearing claim is precisely that per-cell prediction is needed to satisfy a
\emph{spatially-varying} absolute anchor without the single-scalar drift to
$0.092$, \emph{not} that the value is independently identified. We report this as
validation within a wide band
(${\sim}0.05$ vs.\ Carroll's $0.0425$, the same ${\sim}{\pm}40\%$ Cal band),
alongside two caveats: Carroll's single global value is itself under-constrained,
and the intensive closure gap (b) caps any \emph{joint} multi-AOI claim. We do
\emph{not} claim this validates the specific value $0.0425$.

\subsection{Equifinality: fitting the observable is not identifying the parameter}
\label{sec:results-equifinality}

A synthetic experiment with a spatially-varying ground truth confirms the
mechanism behind the ablation. There, a per-cell DINN drives the loss to
${\sim}10^{-4}$ yet recovers \texttt{alpfe} with an \emph{arbitrary sign} across
seeds ($|r|\approx0.9$, sign-flip, $n=6$ seeds; $3$ of $6$ seeds recovered
\texttt{alpfe} with negative sign---so the magnitude correlation is high but the
direction is arbitrary): an excellent fit to the observable
coexists with non-identification of the parameter. This synthetic truth carries
\emph{no} absolute-magnitude anchor on \texttt{alpfe}, so only its sign is free;
it is the real GEOTRACES anchor (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-iron}), present at $n=10$,
that pins \texttt{alpfe}'s magnitude. We place this $n=6$ count adjacent to the
$n=10$ real counts so the difference in seed budget is visible. Identifiability is
parameter-specific---\texttt{Smallgrow}, which has a direct monotonic effect on
the observable, recovers far more consistently in the same test. This is the
synthetic analogue of the real-data finding that \texttt{scav\_rat} and
$R_{\mathrm{PIC:POC}}$ need per-cell structure and absolute anchors to be pinned.

\subsection{Method isolation: the self-twin recovers all six}
\label{sec:results-selftwin}

To separate intrinsic degeneracy from optimization difficulty, we run the same
machinery against box-\emph{generated} targets at the known Carroll optimum.
Started at the truth, it recovers all six parameters at loss ${\sim}10^{-10}$.
The method is therefore sound; the from-mid-bounds stall observed elsewhere is an
optimization/initialization artifact, not an intrinsic property of the parameter
set.

\subsection{Grazing observability remains open}
\label{sec:results-diatomgraz}

\texttt{diatomgraz} is not recovered from the available real observations (best
${\sim}4/10$, indistinguishable from chance); it is recoverable in principle from
dense Darwin POSi/TRAC16 fields, which we have not staged here.

\subsection{Relation to prior work}
\label{sec:results-related}

The closest prior design is the Biogeochemistry-Informed Neural Network of
Xu et al.~\cite{xu2025binn}, the mature originator of the per-location-NN-through-a-%
differentiable-BGC-model architecture. BINN differentiates the \emph{actual}
model (CLM5) and therefore has \emph{no surrogate gap}. Our contribution is
threefold: (i) we transfer the design to a \emph{non}-differentiable GCM
(ECCO-Darwin) via a 0-D differentiable surrogate, which introduces and forces us
to characterize the surrogate gap of Sec.~\ref{sec:results-gap}; (ii) we adopt an
identifiability-\emph{decomposition} lens---asking which parameters are observable
and why---rather than a self-consistency framing; and (iii) we drive
identifiability from real, absolute anchors with multi-AOI attribution. We do
\emph{not} use the native inverse machinery of ECCO---adjoint/4D-Var
\cite{heimbach2015,menemenlis2005} and Green's-functions calibration
\cite{carroll2022}---and we read our results through the sloppiness/%
identifiability lineage of Transtrum and Sethna~\cite{transtrum2012}.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the result is.}
We report neither a calibration of ECCO-Darwin nor a discovery of new
ocean-biogeochemical parameter values, but a \emph{parameter-specific
identifiability map}. Given a coupled GCM (ECCO-Darwin v05) that cannot itself be
differentiated, a 0-D differentiable surrogate---the
\texttt{carroll6\_5pft\_2layer} box---fit to the GCM's output \emph{and} to real
ocean observations identifies a characterizable subset of the GCM's six Carroll-6
calibration parameters. The demonstrated-observable set is the trio
$\{$\texttt{alpfe}, \texttt{scav\_rat}, \texttt{R\_PICPOC}$\}$;
\texttt{diatomgraz} is observable in principle but not from the staged data; and
the growth pair $\{$\texttt{Smallgrow}, \texttt{Biggrow}$\}$ is unobservable by
construction. The mechanism is a per-cell DINN whose predictions pass through the
differentiable box, so one backward pass yields all six exact gradients
\emph{of the surrogate objective}---an inversion-side replacement for the $\sim$7
forward Green's-functions runs, inheriting the surrogate gap and \emph{not} a
like-for-like speedup of the GCM calibration. The central evidence is the
architecture ablation (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-ablation}): with only the parameter
source varied, the per-cell DINN holds the trio 7/10 jointly versus 0/10 for a
global scalar, because a single global vector cannot match spatially varying
absolute anchors. Within that result, \texttt{alpfe} recovers 10/10 in both arms
(mass-balance-identified, its magnitude pinned by the iron anchor), so the
load-bearing per-cell dependence is specifically for in-band recovery of
\texttt{scav\_rat} and \texttt{R\_PICPOC}---and the per-cell \texttt{R\_PICPOC}
value is anchor-imposed to $\approx0.05$, not independently identified.

\paragraph{The single biggest limitation, stated up front.}
This is a \emph{single-method, single-model} study. We run no independent
inversion---no Green's-functions, Bayesian, or 4D-Var \cite{heimbach2015}
optimization---on the same targets, and we perform no forward-Darwin held-out
validation. Consequently, every ``recovery'' is graded against Carroll's
\emph{own} published parameter values \cite{carroll2022} at our generous,
pre-registered $40\%$ Cal-grade band, and is therefore a \emph{consistency check},
not a cross-validated discovery. This is the single largest caveat on the entire
result and we place no claim above it. Compounding limitations: the 0-D box is a
1-degree proxy for a 3-D GCM; targets are a 23-year climatology rather than
time-resolved fields; the study runs on a single-GPU prototype; the intensive
closure gap (b) caps any joint multi-AOI \texttt{R\_PICPOC} claim;
\texttt{diatomgraz} observability is open; and the growth pair is unobservable by
construction.

\paragraph{What the method is useful for.}
As an estimator the machinery is sound: a self-twin against box-generated targets
at the known optimum recovers all six parameters at loss $\sim$$10^{-10}$ from
truth (Sec.~\ref{sec:results-selftwin}), so the from-mid-bounds stall on real
targets is an optimization/initialization artifact, not intrinsic degeneracy. The
useful product is therefore a cheap, differentiable inversion-side gradient
estimator and the parameter-specific identifiability map it produces: it tells a
calibrator \emph{which} Carroll-6 parameters a real-anchored surrogate inversion
can constrain (the iron magnitude via mass balance, the rain ratio via a real
calcite anchor) and which it cannot (the growth pair, and---from the staged
data---grazing), before committing expensive full-GCM Green's-functions effort.
Relative to BINN \cite{xu2025binn}, which differentiates the actual model (CLM5)
and so has no surrogate gap, our delta is to carry this design to a
\emph{non-differentiable} GCM through a surrogate, to adopt an
identifiability-decomposition rather than a self-consistency lens, and to drive
identifiability from real absolute anchors with multi-AOI attribution; we read the
parameter-specific result through the sloppiness/identifiability lineage
\cite{transtrum2012}. Every number is re-derived from raw outputs and gated by
\texttt{verify\_run.py}, a \emph{data-integrity} gate (not scientific validation);
conclusions were pre-registered, verification was adversarial, and two
self-corrections remain on the record.

\paragraph{Forward work.}
The decisive next steps follow directly from the limitations. First, an
\emph{independent inversion}---Green's-functions, Bayesian, or 4D-Var
\cite{heimbach2015}---on the same targets, to convert the consistency check into a
cross-validated result. Second, \emph{forward-Darwin held-out} validation,
propagating recovered parameters through the full GCM on unseen conditions. Third,
\emph{time-resolved} targets in place of the 23-year climatology, to expose the
growth-pair succession handle. Fourth, \emph{dense biogenic-silica} (POSi/TRAC16)
supervision to close the open \texttt{diatomgraz} question. None of these removes
the intensive closure gap (b)---the two-sided, opposite-sign AOI spread that no
single scalar can straddle---which remains a structural cap on any joint multi-AOI
\texttt{R\_PICPOC} claim and motivates a higher-dimensional surrogate as the
longer-term path.

project convention, not a Carroll-stated tolerance.

Three areas of interest (equatorial Pacific, North Atlantic subpolar, Southern

Southern-Ocean artifact with target mean $\approx4.7\times10^{7}$ that otherwise
collapses the ratio normalizer). Optimizer Adam; $1500$ epochs; $n{=}10$ seeds
($0$--$9$); the synthetic equifinality control uses $n{=}6$ seeds. The estimator is
the two-layer five-PFT differentiable box \texttt{carroll6\_5pft\_2layer}; the
ablation control is \texttt{GlobalScalarNet} (one global Carroll-6 vector).

\paragraph{Software, hardware, and integrity gate.}
PyTorch $2.11$ (CUDA $12.8$), Python $3.12$; runs executed on NU Explorer GPUs
(H200 / A100 / T4). Every recovery count is re-derived from raw run output by
\texttt{verify\_run.py}, which recomputes each statistic from raw and exits zero
only on agreement (a data-integrity gate, not a validation of the science).
