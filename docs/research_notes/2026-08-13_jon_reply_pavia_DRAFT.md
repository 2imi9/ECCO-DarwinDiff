One correction on my side, and it is on the Black question you said looked fine.

I described that flux as my sink side anchor. It is not, and I had that wrong. In the box at steady
state the iron leaving the surface layer equals the iron coming in, so the total export flux is set
by the deposition term. It does not respond to the scavenging rate at all. I checked it directly
and a sixteen fold sweep of scavenging moves that flux by zero. So it behaves as a second source
constraint, not as the sink partner I wanted. It is not used in any fit at the moment.

Your answer about taking the flux and skipping the residence time still holds. I just had the wrong
label on it. You were reviewing my description rather than the code, so the mistake was mine to
catch, and I would rather flag it than let it sit.

It also lines up with something else I found this week. A source side constraint cannot move the
scavenging rate at all, and I now have three separate ways of seeing that. So the sink side really
does need its own observation, which is why the paired station question matters more than I first
thought.
